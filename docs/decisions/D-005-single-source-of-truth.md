# D-005: Single source of geometric truth

- Date: 2026-10-04
- Status: Proposed

## Decision

CAD is the source of meshes, `config/hand_model.yaml` is the source of
kinematics, and the URDF is generated by script.

## Alternatives

- Hand-maintained URDF alongside CAD.
- A single CAD export driving everything.

## Rationale

Duplicated geometry drifts. The original spec had H4 = 34 mm vs H6 = 28 mm for
two fingers declared identical.

## Accepted cost

A generator script to maintain and keep in sync with the CAD convention.

## Revisit if

The CAD tool can export kinematics directly and reliably.
