# D-004: Everything public, including tactile research

- Date: 2026-10-04
- Status: Accepted

## Decision

Publish hardware, software, and tactile sensing research in the same public
repository.

## Alternatives

- Keep tactile research in a separate private repository.

## Rationale

The goal is adoption and visibility. A separate private repository would
recreate the synchronisation problem that D-001 avoids.

## Accepted cost

Competitors see the work early.

## Revisit if

A co-authored paper requires anonymity, or a partner imposes confidentiality.
